# Data attribution

The administrative boundary polygons embedded in `data/province.geojson`,
`data/amphoe.geojson`, and `data/tambon.geojson` are derived from Thailand's
Common Operational Dataset — Administrative Boundaries (COD-AB), published on
the [Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX)](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-tha):

> Thailand administrative level 0-3 boundaries (COD-AB). Sourced from the
> Royal Thai Survey Department. Vetting, configuration, and geoservices
> provision by Information Technology Outreach Services (ITOS), University
> of Georgia, with funding from USAID.

This dataset is published under the **Creative Commons Attribution for
Intergovernmental Organisations license (CC BY-IGO)**. Under that license
you're free to share and adapt the data, including commercially, as long as
you:

- give appropriate credit (as above),
- indicate that changes were made — this copy has been geometrically
  simplified from the original for smaller file size, and re-packaged as
  GeoJSON grouped by province/district for use with this library,
- don't suggest HDX, ITOS, USAID, or the Royal Thai Survey Department
  endorse you or your use of the data.

embeds `data/*.geojson`), keep this file alongside it.
